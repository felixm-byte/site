## FAQ

<Callout sx={{ mx: [null, null, -3, -4] }}>
<FAQ>

- **How much does it cost to enter?**
  Nothing! Registration is free, with prizes hopefully available. More information on this will be available soon.

- **Who can participate? 🔭**
  Any students aged 13-18 can join. If you're outside of that range, please get in touch.

- **What can I make? 🤖**
  Anything, as long as it has the potential to do good! A website, app, game, hardware hack, you name it. Judges will rate projects on potential positive impact (how much good your idea could do) and implementation quality (how usable, accessible and advanced your solution is).

- **What if I’m new to coding? 👾**
  No problem! Learn as you go with our introductory workshops & tutorials.

- **Do I need a team? 👨‍👧‍👧**
  No, you'll be able to meet potential team-mates, and form a team of up to 4, or you can work on a project alone.


</FAQ>
